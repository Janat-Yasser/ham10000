# HAM10000: first look

- **10,015 training images** of **7,470 lesions**; 1,512 images in the official test set.
- **4,501 training images (45%) belong to a lesion photographed more than once** (up to 6 photos of one lesion). Split by image, and these siblings end up on both sides.
- Lesions whose photos carry different labels: 0.
